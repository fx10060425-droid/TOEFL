【偏鄉發展與政府預算配置】Rural Infrastructure vs. Direct Cash Subsidies

Professor Gupta: Many rural towns are experiencing severe population decline and aging demographics. Some policymakers argue that the central government should offer direct financial incentives (cash handouts or tax breaks) to individuals and businesses that relocate to rural areas. Others believe the government should instead invest solely in upgrading rural public infrastructure and digital connectivity. Which approach is more effective and why?

Paul (支持直接發錢/減稅): Money talks. Immediate tax cuts or housing subsidies lower living costs right away and stimulate the local economy fastest.

Claire (支持升級基建與網路): Handing out cash is a temporary fix. Once the subsidy ends, people leave. Investing in high-speed internet, hospitals, and public transit naturally attracts remote workers and industries over time.
